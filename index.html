Quotax: bid control for estimators

Thin-margin bids can't leave the building without a named sign-off.

  • Margin floor per bid. Set the floor on each bid (scope and risk vary), not one number for every job.
  • 🚩 THIN JOB flag. Internal cost + markup on every line. When margin drops below the floor, the bid is flagged with the dollar shortfall and each thin section is called out.
  • Send gate. A bid under its floor is held: print and share are locked until an approver who isn't the estimator signs off with name and reason. Every approval is time-stamped and logged.
  • Nothing to implement. No take-off, BIM or login. Estimators open a web page and type the lines. Itemized sections, subtotals and totals that foot, with costs and margins never on the customer's copy.
